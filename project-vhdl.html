The challenge

A processor is not one component. It is a collection of smaller building blocks that must behave correctly together across every clock cycle.

02 / 03

How it works

The architecture brings together registers, an ALU, program control and memory or I/O interfaces. VHDL describes the hardware and simulations help verify its behavior.

03 / 03

What I learned

Hardware design rewards precise thinking, clear interfaces and methodical testing. Even a small change can affect the entire datapath.
