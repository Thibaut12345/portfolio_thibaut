01 / 03

The challenge

Pylos may look simple, but each turn opens up many possible futures. The challenge is to pick a strong move without spending too long searching every possible continuation.

02 / 03

How it works

The bot explores a tree of future game states. Search techniques such as minimax and alpha-beta pruning help evaluate alternatives and avoid work that cannot change the final decision.

03 / 03

What's interesting

A stronger evaluation function, smart move ordering and efficient state handling can matter as much as simply searching deeper.
